Network of Hashtags
This network visualizes the relationships between 763 hashtags collected from Instagram posts featuring the female body.

The dataset was collected starting from #woman and following the flow of posts suggested by the platform, selecting only those where the female body was the main subject.

Each node is a hashtag. Edges connect hashtags that appear together in the same post.

The hashtags extracted from these posts were then analysed and grouped into categories↘, revealing the different narratives and topics associated with the female body within the platform.

Select a category↘ on the right to explore its hashtags. Zoom in to reveal more names, or pause over a node to see its nearest connections. Click a node to hold its connections, or switch to All links to see every relationship.
